# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People authoring and presenting polished decks, including agents working under
human supervision. Authors need direct manipulation, predictable undo, and
portable files; presenters need dependable keyboard, touch, and second-screen
controls in focused live settings.

## Product Purpose

bento makes office documents self-contained, durable HTML files whose viewer,
editor, content, and optional collaboration capabilities travel together. The
product succeeds when documents remain locally owned and recoverable while
human and agent edits feel capable, transparent, and safe.

## Brand Personality

Capable, calm, and transparent. bento should feel like a serious creative tool
that stays out of the author's way while making powerful behavior inspectable.

## Anti-references

- Opaque AI magic that replaces broad document state without explaining or
  scoping its changes.
- Cloud-first tools that make local ownership, offline use, or recovery feel
  secondary.
- Toy presentation editors with decorative controls, surprising navigation,
  or unreliable live-show behavior.
- Motion added for spectacle rather than communication or state.

## Design Principles

- The file is the product: preserve identity, portability, and graceful
  behavior without services.
- Powerful actions remain targeted, inspectable, undoable, and safe under
  concurrent work.
- Practice directness: familiar controls, stable identities, and evidence over
  hidden heuristics.
- Motion communicates hierarchy, continuity, and presenter intent.
- Human and agent authoring use the same document model and quality bar.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and touch interactions need equivalent outcomes. Reduced-motion
preferences must preserve content order and presenter control while removing
non-essential animation. Controls require clear state, focus behavior, and
localized labels; color must not be the only status signal.
